05RemotePass · 2024–25Content & storytellingThe service →

A content engine that turned search into revenue.

RemotePass helps companies hire, manage and pay teams in 150+ countries. When I joined in early 2024, it had just raised $5.5M, but nothing tied content to leads, adoption or revenue. I built its first content strategy across the whole customer journey: how people find us, how they talk to sales, and why they stay.

Q1 2025 alone$192Kin revenue from people who found RemotePass through search and social content, engaged and converted, with no ad spend.
174%rise in organic user acquisition within a year

Four pillars, one strategy

01 · SEO-driven acquisition

Ranking for what buyers actually search

Search-optimised articles built to capture high-intent traffic where RemotePass had little visibility, from hiring strategy to Saudization rules.

#1 on Google for “how SMEs can compete for top talent”, and on page one for “Saudization requirements for employers”.

02 · Thought leadership

Credibility in a regulated space

Expert insight and HR policy breakdowns that earned mentions in key newsletters and media. I edited pieces for our leadership and brought in outside experts.

An article by CEO Kamal Reggad, edited by me, featured on Wamda; and a RemotePass piece with insight from Toggl’s CEO, Alari Aho.

03 · Product education

Content that keeps customers

Help articles, walkthrough videos and Q&A posts that showed users how to get more out of the platform, to improve retention.

A Q&A video with the product lead on Local Payroll, and the Help Center I helped build.

04 · Sales enablement

Material that closes deals

Pitch decks, one-pagers and customer case studies built with the sales team, to speed up the sales cycle and help close more deals.

Customer stories from Pemo, Podeo and Lean.
