EmpowerQ

Every queer Nigerian professional deserves a fair shot at a good career.

EmpowerQ is a nonprofit building economic inclusion for LGBTQ+ professionals in Nigeria: career support, honest research and a community that makes work safer and more rewarding.

Get involved ↓ Visit EmpowerQ ↗

Why it exists

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn’t.

My career started because a friend told me about an opportunity in a law lecture. One open door changed everything that came after it. Since 2020 I’ve tried to open doors for others: Digivention, Write to Win, and a career newsletter read by 900+ people.

But some people need more than a newsletter. Many LGBTQ+ Nigerians are shut out of the networks, mentorship and fair hiring that careers depend on, and too often they have to choose between being safe and being seen. In 2025, I co-founded EmpowerQ with Alexandria to change that.

We focus on what moves a career: jobs, skills, research that makes the case to employers, and a community where people don’t have to hide to get ahead.

— Jeremiah Ajayi, Executive Director and Co-Founder

What we’re building

Practical support at every step of a career.

Flagship · Social Shifters 2026 finalist

Lavender Ladder

An employment programme built for progression, not just placement. Participants get mentorship from working professionals, career coaching and a route into recruiter-ready talent pools with employers who want to hire inclusively.
