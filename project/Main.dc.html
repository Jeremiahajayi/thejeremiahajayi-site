Letter from the editor

How I got here

Candid photo — Lagos

In 2018, I was sitting in a law lecture I barely remember when a friend told me about freelance writing. That conversation changed everything.

I went from ghostwriting blog posts to content strategy to product marketing at PiggyVest, Stears and RemotePass, then led product and content marketing at an AI visibility platform. The pattern was the same everywhere: companies weren't short on marketing activity. They were short on marketing that connected to revenue. That gap became the thing I learned to close.

In 2025 I co-founded EmpowerQ, Nigeria's first professional empowerment organisation for LGBTQ+ communities. It is the most important work I have done, and it has made me a sharper operator in every other room I walk into.

— Jeremiah
