042023 to 2026

Starting again, and going global

Leaving OAU left a gap that mattered to me. In 2023 I went back to school, studying Business Management at Miva Open University while working full-time.

At RemotePass, a Dubai-based HR and payroll platform, I led GTM for launches across EMEA, North America and APAC. Some went out in English and Arabic on the same day; one partnership reached 270+ media outlets. The work took me to more than ten countries.

Then I led product marketing at Xofu, an AI visibility platform, and started publishing my own research on how AI assistants choose which brands to recommend.
