The short version

I was meant to be a lawyer.

In 2018, in a law lecture I barely remember, a friend told me about freelance writing. I wrote my way into strategy, then into marketing for some of Africa’s most watched startups, then for teams around the world. Good ideas, it turns out, really do go places.

Along the way I kept opening doors for others: Digivention, a career conference for 1,000+ young Nigerians; Write to Win, for freelance writers; and Wednesday Goodness, a career newsletter read by 900+ people. EmpowerQ is where that became a mission.
