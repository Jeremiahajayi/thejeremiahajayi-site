01Stears · 2023Content & storytelling

How a new election tracker beat the BBC on Google

Stears rebuilt Nigeria’s first real-time election tracker for the 2023 general election. As the content and SEO lead, my job was to make sure millions of Nigerians could find it, and trust it, the moment results started coming in.

I worked across editorial, engineering, data and design.

  1. Built the search strategy from scratch for questions like “2023 election results” and “presidential candidates Nigeria”.
  2. Designed programmatic candidate profiles: 10,000+ pages covering every candidate for governor, senate and president.
  3. Trained the analyst team, mostly economists, to publish for search, and kept pages accurate and fast at peak traffic.

#1on Google for “2023 election results”, above the BBC, CNN and INEC

1.9M+users, 52% of them from search
